{%- comment -%} The SESS clip launcher. The whole 8x8 grid is patterns: column = track (hue per track: red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple, pink), row = pattern (81-88 = pattern 1 of tracks 1-8 ... 11-18 = pattern 8). The right column is the 8 scenes. UP = DUP, DOWN = CLR, RIGHT = song start/stop. Params: patterns 8 comma-separated strings of 8 chars, pattern 1 first. Each char is one track: . empty f filled p playing (active pattern) q queued (blinks) Example (patterns 1-3 only, the rest empty): "p.p.....,f.f.p...,........" (missing rows stay empty) scenes 8 chars, scene 1 first: . empty f filled c last launched q queued s song entry a morph A b morph B song off (default) | ready | playing : the RIGHT arrow (song start/stop) hint, pads, blink, held, alt, caption, numbers {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign cl_rows = include.patterns | default: "" | remove: " " | split: "," -%} {%- assign cl_hues =
